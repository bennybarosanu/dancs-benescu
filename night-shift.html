2024 · 70 min · Theatre-dance hybrid · 14+

Tura de noapte
Night Shift

Two strangers experience a night together during which nothing is what it seems — not even time.

Scroll

Mircea and Irina, the two characters of the piece, meet and fall in love when neither of them has anything left to lose. Caught in a borderline situation, they live the typical "one-night stand" on an atypical night — one in which there is no longer present, past, or future, life or death, dream or reality, but only doors opened between them all, corridors that become pretexts for melancholic, existentialist, and comic thematic tensions.

The lines are music, the light is disco, and the bodies pulsate on an army of techno bass, creating a hypnotic performative hybrid that reveals itself little by little. Prepare yourself for a dancephonic-theatre show — a moving sensation, between adrenaline and emotion, a story in which we can all recognize ourselves, but where nothing is what it seems.

The two are "free prisoners" of a world that is ritualistic in meaning but entirely contemporary in the elements that make it up. The text, written by Mihai Ivașcu, is delivered in an almost Brechtian register, against jerky dance movement, small believable compulsions and incomplete physical mirrorings.

Night Shift premiered in February 2024 at Casa Kerim and was selected the same year for the 34th Romanian National Theatre Festival and for Bucharest Fringe, where Alexandra Mihaela Dancs received the Special Jury Prize for bodily expressiveness in the role of Irina.
