2025 · 70 min · Dance & multimedia performance

triluREELu

The plunge of two performers into a phone locked on reels — where the feed is on stage and the bodies scroll back.

Scroll

triluREELu is the plunge of two performers into a phone locked on the most diverse reels — from viral dances to motivational speeches. As they "swim" through the virtual immensity, the show becomes absurdly comic and technologically complex: the feed is on stage, digital scenography and bodies interact in hybrid 3D reels, captions and crowded music become language, vanity and rhythm grow into a frenzy.

Ephemerality, fragmented and random realities, high-speed stimuli, plus other dynamics of reel culture, are reinterpreted and recontextualized with humor and irony, in a choreographic-performative-multimedia puzzle that puts a finger on the screen of society.

The piece is built entirely out of small fragments that follow one another at a maddening speed, obeying no rule other than the rule of the algorithm. What it produces is a state the project's documentarist calls postcoherence — a condition in which logical connections have come apart.

triluREELu is part of The Body In REEL Time, a project of Art No More Association, conceived by Vlad Benescu and Alexandra Mihaela Dancs, co-financed by AFCN in 2025. The project choreographically explores corporeality in the era of reels and examines, with humor and a critical eye, a world full of virtual dopamine and fingers tired of scrolling. It was accompanied throughout by the research and documentation of artist and anthropologist Rucsandra Pop, and by a series of public debates.
