2023 · Performance

Somatic Freaks 1.0

An internal reality, difficult to perceive from the outside — narrated out loud, in real time.

Scroll

Their somatic practice has made its way into everything that involves movement — domestic, sporting, or artistic. They filter everything through it, the practice becoming a permanent process of training, maintenance, and recovery that constantly evolves through ongoing recycling of somatic sensations and knowledge. Not an obsession, but from the outside, the two could appear as Somatic Freaks.

Somatic Freaks 1.0 reveals an internal reality difficult to perceive from the outside. The two continuously describe what they do and feel happening inside with their musculoskeletal structures, entering a dialogue generated by conscious movement and the verbalization of every detail observed physically.

This is the first version of the research — the point where the practice stopped being only a private, daily routine and became material for an audience. A year later it grew into the three-hour durational performance Somatic Freaks — The Proprioceptors Revolution, where the same attention to balance moved from language into a large tandem balancing apparatus.
