Luați de VAL...s

Their first piece as a duo — made for a house, and for the way a wave keeps reforming instead of ending.

Scroll

Luați de VAL...s (2022) is the first dance performance created by Alexandra Mihaela Dancs and Vlad Benescu — performed at Casa Kerim, inspired by its architecture and history. Beyond the house on Parfumului 19, the creative process was nourished during the summer by working with the waves of the Black Sea, through long surfing sessions. Being "taken by the wave" became, for them, artistic practice.

Closeness and conflict. Negotiation and complicity. Tenderness and risk. Chaos. Energy.

The piece is site-specific in the strict sense: it happens in all the rooms of a single-storey house in the old Jewish quarter of Bucharest, for an audience of around twenty people, with the light operated live by the performers themselves.

The dynamics between us have their own initiative, their own path, they take us where they want, just like the waves that always seek the shore — not to end there, but to continually reform. We chose to throw ourselves in and let ourselves be carried.

Luați de VAL...s premiered in October 2022 at Casa Kerim — Hearth, where it continues to be presented as part of the Hearth season. In 2023 it was part of CNDB Itinerant (the National Centre for Dance Bucharest), and in 2024 it was invited to Bucharest Collage Festival. It has passed twenty performances.
