2025 · 70 min

triluREELu

Scroll ↓

The plunge of two performers into a phone locked on the most diverse reels — from viral dances to motivational speeches.

The feed is on stage, digital scenography and bodies interact in hybrid 3D reels, captions and crowded music become language, vanity and rhythm grow into a frenzy. The piece is built entirely of small fragments following one another at a maddening speed, obeying no rule other than the rule of the algorithm.

2024 · Durational · 3 hours

Somatic Freaks
The Proprioceptors Revolution

Scroll ↓

Three hours of two bodies negotiating with a large tandem balancing apparatus — a living laboratory of failure, recovery and trust.

Their physicality adapts to its specific mechanics in a choreography of subtle movements. An endless dialogue is created between the ephemerality of stability and the unpredictability of moments of unbalance. The audience is free to come and go: what you see depends on when you arrive.

2024 · 70 min · 14+

Tura de noapte
Night Shift

Scroll ↓

Two strangers experience a night together during which nothing is what it seems — not even time.

The lines are music, the light is disco, and the bodies pulsate on an army of techno bass. Mircea and Irina meet and fall in love when neither of them has anything left to lose, in a hypnotic performative hybrid the company calls dancephonic-theatre.

2022 · Site-specific · Debut

Luați de VAL...s

Scroll ↓

Their first piece as a duo — made for a house, and for the way a wave keeps reforming instead of ending.

Performed in all the rooms of a single-storey house in the old Jewish quarter, for around twenty people, with the light operated live by the performers. The creative process was nourished during the summer by long surfing sessions on the Black Sea.

2023 · Performance

Somatic Freaks 1.0

Scroll ↓

An internal reality, difficult to perceive from the outside — narrated out loud, in real time.

The two continuously describe what they feel happening inside their musculoskeletal structures, entering a dialogue generated by conscious movement and the verbalization of every physical detail. This is where the Somatic Freaks research began.
